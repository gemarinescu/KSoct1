BEFORE YOU DRAW

Good millwork starts before the first line.

The course is structured around the questions that prevent expensive revisions later. Every module moves from visual judgment to technical clarity.

01

What is the room asking for?

Read scale, sight lines, circulation, ceiling height, light, and the visual weight of surrounding elements.

02

What should disappear?

Learn where restraint matters - reveals, shadow lines, concealed functions, and details that keep the composition calm.

03

What must be resolved?

Work through dimensions, appliance clearances, hardware, fillers, panels, seams, and edge conditions before fabrication.

04

How will it be built?

Connect design intent to real materials, cabinet construction, installation tolerances, sequencing, and site realities.

05

How will it be communicated?

Create clearer drawings and specifications so the fabricator, installer, designer, and client are seeing the same thing.

06

What makes it feel finished?

Refine proportion, alignment, repetition, lighting, hardware, and transitions until the room reads as one idea.

THE DIFFERENCE

Beautiful is not enough. It has to resolve.

Reference images can show you what you like. They do not teach you how the room works.

Millwork School is built around the decisions between the image and the finished room - proportion, detailing, materials, coordination, and execution.

ABOUT THE SCHOOL

A practical design education without the noise.

The platform is being developed as a focused learning environment for people who want to understand custom millwork beyond surface-level inspiration.

Lessons are intended to be revisited. References should stay useful. The goal is simple: build judgment that carries from one project to the next.
